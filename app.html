The team on duty referees and writes the score on paper. You enter it yourself from Schedule or Courts. Student accounts can only read the tournament — there is no way for a student to record, change or submit a score.

Rules in play

  • Every match is doubles — pairs are drawn from within each team.
  • Rally scoring: a point is awarded on every rally, whoever serves.
  • Game to 11, win by 2, hard cap 15.
  • Teams switch ends when a side first reaches 6.
  • Serve passes to the other side whenever the receiving side wins the rally.
  • Partners rotate: no student is paired with the same teammate twice.
  • Team tie = 3 doubles games; the team winning most games takes the tie.
